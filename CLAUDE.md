# Writing preferences (from Katelyn)

These apply to all promotional and site content: self-promotion (portfolio, résumé) and products or services (Web Deva).

- **No sentence-style subheadings or lead lines.** Don't put a big phrase under a heading such as "I start with how the work moves, then decide what a tool should do" or "I do X so you can Y". She hates this pattern: it reads as unprofessional and doesn't sound human.
- Headings are short labels, not slogans or statements.
- Put the substance in plain body copy at normal size: concrete, specific and in her own voice, with no marketing flourish.
