What is included with registration?
Registration includes full access to all sessions and programming, breakfast and lunch each day, refreshment breaks, networking events throughout the conference, and all evening events.
When does early bird pricing end?
Early bird pricing is available through January 22, 2027.
Do I need to be a member to receive the member rate?
To qualify for the member rate at the 2027 conference, your organization must have an active 2027 membership. Any attendees who use the member discount code without an active 2027 membership prior to the conference will be billed the difference between the member and non-member attendee rates.
What is the cancellation policy?
We will accept cancellation requests that are received by 11:59pm January 10, 2027. Cancellations made on or before the deadline will be refunded, minus a $25 administrative processing fee. Please contact us with any questions.

Photography and Media Policy

Registration and participation in the 2027 AdNet Annual Conference constitutes agreement to the use of your image or voice in recordings, photographs, and other reproductions of conference activities by AdNet. If you have concerns about being recorded, please notify conference staff upon arrival.

Questions About Registration?

We are happy to help. Reach out to us directly and a member of our team will be in touch shortly.
