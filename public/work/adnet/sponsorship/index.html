Why Sponsor AdNet 2027?

AdNet is the premier networking and professional development resource for professionals serving in fund development, philanthropic advising, and donor services. Our membership includes over 175 top professionals working in community foundations large and small, from urban to rural, across the country. Sponsoring AdNet 2027 puts your organization directly in front of senior decision-makers who shape philanthropic programs managing billions in charitable assets.

Building on two consecutive sold-out conferences, AdNet 2027 takes place March 22–24, 2027 at the Marriott Chicago Downtown Magnificent Mile in Chicago, IL.

Dedicated Audience

71% of AdNet attendees hold donor-facing or fundraising roles -- philanthropic advisors, donor services, gift planning, and development officers. No traffic from unrelated sectors.

Senior Decision-Makers

Over 60% of attendees are at the senior management level or above, including executive leadership and C-suite from community foundations nationwide.

National Reach

Attendees represent community foundations across all four regions of the country, managing collectively over $72 billion in charitable assets.
