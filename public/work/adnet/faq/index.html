AdNet 2027 Annual Conference

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often about the AdNet 2027 Annual Conference. If you don't find what you're looking for, use the inquiry form on the most relevant conference page — Registration, Hotel & Travel, Call for Sessions, or Sponsorship — and our team will follow up.

Registration

When does registration open, and what are the rates?

Registration for the AdNet 2027 Annual Conference is open now. Member rates are $825 (early bird) and $925 (regular). Non-member rates are $1,250 (early bird) and $1,350 (regular). Early bird pricing is available through January 22, 2027.

What does my registration fee include?

Your registration includes full access to all sessions and programming, breakfast and lunch each day, refreshment breaks, networking opportunities throughout the conference, and all evening receptions and social events.

Do I need to be a member to receive the member rate?

To qualify for the member rate at the 2027 conference, your organization must have an active 2027 membership. Any attendees who use the member discount code without an active 2027 membership prior to the conference will be billed the difference between the member and non-member attendee rates.

Will I receive a confirmation or receipt of my registration?

Yes. After you complete your registration, you'll receive a confirmation and receipt by email. You can also confirm your registration status at any time through your member portal.

What if I have a budget or timing issue with registering before the early bird deadline?

We'd suggest going ahead and registering now. When you check out, you can choose “Invoice” instead of paying by card — this reserves your spot(s) without requiring immediate payment. Just keep in mind that the refund policy still applies once registrations are submitted. If any of your team members can't attend, please notify us by the January 22, 2027 cancellation deadline to cancel. After that date, a substitute can be sent in their place at no additional cost.

Is there a group registration discount available?

For questions about registering multiple attendees from your organization, please use the registration inquiry form and our team will be happy to help.

Cancellations, Refunds & Changes

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

We will accept cancellation requests that are received by 11:59pm January 10, 2027. Cancellations made on or before the deadline will be refunded, minus a $25 administrative processing fee. Please contact us with any questions.

Where can I update my registration or change attendees?

We understand that things change unexpectedly, so transferring your registration to another staff member within your organization is an option, at no additional cost. Just submit the change through the registration inquiry form, and please give us as much notice as possible so that we can plan accordingly.

Hotel & Travel

The room block is full — will more rooms be added?

Our room block at the Marriott Chicago Downtown Magnificent Mile is limited and available on a first-come, first-served basis. If the block fills before you're able to book, please use the Hotel & Travel inquiry form. While we can't guarantee additional rooms at the group rate, we'll do our best to help you find nearby options.

At the Conference

What is the dress code?

Conference attire is business casual. We recommend bringing a layer, as meeting room temperatures can vary throughout the day.

I'm bringing my family with me — are there activities they can take part in?

Evening receptions and social events are a wonderful part of the AdNet experience. If you're planning to bring family or guests, please use the registration inquiry form so we can share what's included and any options for ticketed events.

When will the conference app be available?

Details about the conference app, including how to download it and when it will be available, will be shared with registered attendees closer to the conference.

General

Who should attend this conference?

The AdNet Annual Conference is designed for community foundation staff at all levels and stages of their careers, from new professionals to seasoned leaders. Colleagues from organizations that partner with or serve community foundations are also welcome.

How can I be a speaker or sponsor at the conference?

Interested in presenting? Visit the Call for Sessions page to submit a proposal. Interested in sponsoring? Visit the Sponsorship page to express interest or ask questions.

Why is the conference scheduled during a religious holiday?

As we plan the conference each year, AdNet is balancing a number of factors, including hotel availability and affordability, location accessibility, the busy seasons of community foundations, religious holidays, and school break calendars. It's a complex process, and while we aim to be as thoughtful and inclusive as possible, some conflicts are unfortunately difficult to avoid.

My question isn't here — who should I contact?

For anything not covered here, use the inquiry form on the most relevant conference page — Registration, Hotel & Travel, Call for Sessions, or Sponsorship — and a member of our team will follow up with you.

Still Have Questions?

Our team is here to help with anything you need before the AdNet 2027 Annual Conference. Send us a note through the registration inquiry form and we'll follow up.
