AdNet 2027 Annual Conference

Call for Sessions

Submission Deadline: October 23, 2026

Submit a Session Proposal for the AdNet 2027 Annual Conference

The AdNet Annual Conference brings together professionals from community foundations across the country to share insights, explore best practices, and learn from one another. As we plan for 2027, we recognize the challenges and rapid shifts facing the philanthropic sector -- and we need forward-thinking sessions that inspire, educate, and equip our attendees.

This year's conference is designed with every career stage and foundation size in mind. AdNet is again collaborating with CommA and will include a communications-focused track open to all attendees.

Call For Sessions Guide
Submit a Session Proposal

Small and Medium Foundations

We are placing extra emphasis on content for smaller and mid-sized community foundations -- sessions with tools and frameworks that scale.

Advanced Practitioner Content

We want deeper sessions for experienced professionals. Please indicate a clear experience level in your proposal -- 101 vs. 201 helps attendees choose the right sessions.

Partnership with CommA

Donor communications sessions in partnership with CommA are open to all attendees. Co-presentations pairing large and small foundation perspectives are especially valued.

What We're Looking For in 2027

Strong proposals should address current and emerging challenges in philanthropy, offer tools or frameworks attendees can adapt and apply, and identify 2–3 specific things attendees will leave knowing or able to do. We also ask that proposals reflect diverse perspectives and foundation sizes, encourage audience participation, indicate a clear experience level (introductory, intermediate, or advanced), and keep the focus educational. Sessions may not serve as sales pitches or product demonstrations.

Topics of interest include:

  • Planned Giving
  • Complex Gifts & Private Foundation Conversions
  • Donor Stewardship & Engagement
  • Business Development & Outreach Tactics
  • Facilitation & Interpersonal Dynamics
  • Professional Advisor Engagement
  • Fund Development & DAF Strategy
  • Advisor Managed Funds & Financial Firm Partnerships
  • Portfolio Segmentation
  • Next-Gen & Family Philanthropy
  • Legal Updates & Issues with Aging Donors
  • Co-investing & Collective Giving
  • Rural & Regional CF Challenges
  • Endowment Campaigns & Fee Models
  • Fundraising for Discretionary Dollars
  • AI Applications, Governance & Emerging Tools
  • Team Development & Succession Planning
  • Burnout & Resilience
  • KPIs & Performance Measurement
  • Technology Tools & Trends
  • DEI in Advancement & Donor Engagement
  • Communications & Marketing Best Practices
  • Partnering with Program Teams to Advance Mission

Who Can Submit

We welcome submissions from community foundation staff at all levels and experience stages.

Non-community foundation organizations: If your organization is not a community foundation, it must be a sponsor at the Thought Leader level or higher to submit a session proposal. See the 2027 sponsorship page for details, or contact Stephen Stephens to discuss sponsorship.

Exceptions: If your organization is not a CF but is similar in mission or scope -- for example, a community-based nonprofit, an individual not representing a company, or a public sector partner -- you are welcome to submit. The committee will review and follow up with you directly.

Speaker logistics: Speakers are expected to cover their own conference registration and travel. AdNet is unable to offer honoraria at this time, though we are exploring ways to enhance the speaker experience.

Have questions about eligibility or submitting a proposal? Use the questions form at the bottom of this page and a member of our team will follow up with you.

Submit Your Proposal

Submit your session idea by October 23, 2026. The AdNet Content Committee reviews all submissions using a standardized rubric. Submitters can expect a decision by December 18, 2026. If you have questions about submitting, use the questions form at the bottom of this page.

Questions About Submitting?

Have questions about submitting a session proposal, eligibility, or the review process? Complete the form below and a member of our team will follow up with you.

When is the submission deadline?
The deadline to submit a session proposal is October 23, 2026. Proposals submitted early receive the most thorough review from the Content Committee.
Can I submit more than one proposal, or co-present?
Yes -- you may submit multiple proposals, and each is reviewed independently. Co-presentations are encouraged, especially sessions that pair perspectives from large and small community foundations. If the committee receives multiple proposals on similar topics, they may reach out to combine them into a panel.
Does presenting include a registration discount?
Speakers are expected to cover their own registration and travel at this time. AdNet is unable to offer honoraria but is exploring ways to enhance the speaker experience. Use the questions form at the bottom of this page with any questions.
