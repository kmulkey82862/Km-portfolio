Travel to Chicago

By Air
Chicago is served by two major airports. O'Hare International Airport (ORD) is 17 miles northwest of downtown and accessible via the CTA Blue Line. Midway Airport (MDW) is 11 miles southwest and accessible via the CTA Orange Line. Both airports offer cab, rideshare, and transit options directly to the hotel.
By Train
Amtrak serves Chicago Union Station at 225 S Canal Street, roughly 1.5 miles from the conference hotel. From Union Station, the hotel is accessible via taxi, rideshare, or a short walk. Chicago is one of the most connected rail hubs in the country.
Local Transit
The hotel is located along the Magnificent Mile, with easy access to the CTA Red Line at Grand station and multiple bus routes on Michigan Avenue. Chicago's transit system makes it straightforward to explore the city without a car throughout your stay.
