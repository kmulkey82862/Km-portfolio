Add-Ons & Engagement

Build on your sponsorship

Speaking, demos and events that put your team in the conversation. Branding add-ons and pricing are in the prospectus.

Open to all

Call for Sessions

Every breakout, workshop, case study and debate is chosen through a merit-based call for sessions, and sponsors compete on equal footing. Strong proposals bring in at least two sectors, rest on evidence, and leave attendees with something they can put to work the week they get home.

Thought Leader and above

“Yes, And…” Improv Panel

Unrehearsed, cross-sector and live in the exhibit hall. Volunteer sponsors and attendees, drawn by lottery, form a panel on the spot and get 10 minutes to diagnose a real chronic disease challenge, propose a solution, name the barriers and outline first steps.

Thought Leader and above, while available

Spark Sessions

A 10-minute featured slot on an Ecosystem Stage inside the exhibit hall. No moderator and no format rules: ten minutes to tell your story the way you want to tell it.

Health tech & wearables

Live product demos

Have a product or wearable to demo live? Tell us when you sign on and we’ll help craft a demo experience. Contact Stephen Stephens at sstephens@arbmeetings.com.

Thought Leader and above

Ancillary events

Host a reception, focus group, hospitality suite, investor meeting or other event alongside ACDC. Events need written approval from ACDC, must fall outside official program hours, and are promoted by the host sponsor; they are not listed in official ACDC materials.

Good to Know

Sponsor and exhibitor policies

The full terms and conditions are in the prospectus.

01What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations received in writing by the cancellation deadline in your sponsorship agreement receive a 50% refund, processed after the conference. Send cancellations to sstephens@arbmeetings.com. There are no refunds after that deadline or for no-shows. Substitutions can be made at any time at no charge.

02When is payment due?

Within 60 days of receiving your invoice, and in full before the conference starts. Sponsorships more than 30 days past due may be canceled so another sponsor can take the spot.

03Can we book adjacent booths?

Yes. Note the request on your booth application and submit all requests together; you can request up to three consecutive spaces. Final assignments are made by conference management. Shared booths are not allowed.

04What happens if we don’t set up on time?

Booths not set up by the installation deadline may be reassigned without a refund.
