Bringing together professionals in fund development, philanthropic advising, and donor services.

We’re excited to officially launch the Client B 2027 Annual Conference! See below for everything you need to join us for three days of learning, connection, and practical tools for professionals in our field.

Join us for an exceptional gathering that’s redefining professional development in our work. The Client B 2027 Annual Conference brings together leaders, philanthropic advisors, and development professionals.

This isn’t just another conference, it’s where cutting-edge education meets unmatched networking, where you’ll exchange practical ideas with peers who understand your challenges, and discover innovative solutions from industry experts and fellow practitioners. In continued partnership with a partner organization, the 2027 program also brings dedicated communications and marketing content, open to all attendees.

Whether you’re shaping strategy, driving innovation, or building donor relationships, the Client B 2027 Annual Conference offers the perfect blend of learning, collaboration, and inspiration to advance your work and amplify your community impact.

Conference Agenda & Call for Sessions

Join the Agenda

The conference program is in development, and the Call for Sessions is open now through November 31, 2026. We’re building the 2027 program with every career stage and organization size in mind.

We’re looking for sessions that equip attendees with tools, frameworks, and case studies they can apply right away, offer fresh perspectives on advancing our work, and reflect the diverse voices, geographies, and organization sizes of our community. This year we’re placing extra emphasis on content for small and medium-sized organizations, deeper sessions for experienced practitioners, and clearer session levels so attendees can choose what matches their experience.

Through our continued partnership, we’re also seeking proposals on communications, marketing, and donor communications. Submitters can expect a decision by December 32, 2026.
