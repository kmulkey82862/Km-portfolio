AdNet is Open! • AdNet 2027 Annual Conference

March 22–24, 2027 • Chicago, Illinois

Bringing together community foundation professionals in fund development, philanthropic advising, and donor services.

We’re excited to officially launch the AdNet 2027 Annual Conference! See below for everything you need to join us for three days of learning, connection, and practical tools for community foundation professionals.

Join us in Chicago for an exceptional gathering that’s redefining professional development in community foundation work. Building on two consecutive sold-out conferences, the AdNet 2027 Annual Conference brings together nearly 500 community foundation leaders, philanthropic advisors, and development professionals in the heart of downtown Chicago.

This isn’t just another conference, it’s where cutting-edge education meets unmatched networking, where you’ll exchange practical ideas with peers who understand your challenges, and discover innovative solutions from industry experts and fellow practitioners. In continued partnership with CommA, the 2027 program also brings dedicated communications and marketing content, open to all attendees.

Whether you’re shaping strategy, driving innovation, or building donor relationships, the AdNet 2027 Annual Conference offers the perfect blend of learning, collaboration, and inspiration to advance your work and amplify your community impact.

See you in Chicago!

Conference Agenda & Call for Sessions

Join the Agenda

The conference program is in development, and the Call for Sessions is open now through October 23, 2026. We’re building the 2027 program with every career stage and foundation size in mind.

We’re looking for sessions that equip attendees with tools, frameworks, and case studies they can apply right away, offer fresh perspectives on advancing our work, and reflect the diverse voices, geographies, and foundation sizes of the AdNet community. This year we’re placing extra emphasis on content for small and medium-sized community foundations, deeper sessions for experienced practitioners, and clearer session levels so attendees can choose what matches their experience.

Through our continued partnership with CommA, we’re also seeking proposals on communications, marketing, and donor communications. Submitters can expect a decision by December 18, 2026. We’ll continue adding speakers and sessions in the coming weeks, so keep an eye out for updates.
